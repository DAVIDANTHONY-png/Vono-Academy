Who is the programme designed for?＋

The programme is designed primarily for 100-level university students who want to build an introductory foundation in programming.

Do I have to register for all three languages?＋

No. You can register for one language, any two languages or all three. The applicable package price is shown above.

Are classes online or physical?＋

The programme uses a hybrid learning format combining physical and online learning. Details of the arrangements will be shared with registered students.

How do I pay and confirm my registration?＋

Pay by bank transfer to the OPay account displayed on this page. Upload your receipt through the registration form. Your payment will be manually checked before it is marked as verified.

When will I receive class details?＋

Class arrangements, venue or online access details, and any additional onboarding information will be communicated to registered students.
